Before you go

Entry rules

Age

Under 18s welcome until 10pm and must be with a guardian; the bars are 18+ with ID checked at the gate.

ID

Bring photo ID — accepted are Taiwan ID, passport or ARC.

Re-entry

allowed until 11pm with your wristband intact; after 11pm, entry is one-time.

Bags

large bags are checked free at the Huaxi Street gate.

Cash

many stalls are cash-only, though the bigger ones take cards and mobile pay.

Weather

the market runs rain or shine and is covered through the main alley.

Accessibility

step-free routes run through Alleys A and B; ask any steward for the lift key to the Lantern Court.

Before you buy

Questions

Can I buy at the gate?
Yes, unless the night is sold out — weekends usually are by Thursday.
Can I change my night?
Single Night Passes can be moved once, free, up to 24 hours before.
Are passes refundable?
Fully refundable up to seven days before the festival; after that, transfer them to a friend.
Do drink tokens expire?
They are valid all three nights, one per token, at any bar stall.
Where do I pick up my wristband?
At the Huaxi Street gate from 5:30pm — bring the QR code from your confirmation email.
