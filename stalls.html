Signature dish

The Dish People Queue 40 Minutes For.

Ah-Cheng's Beef Noodle Soup — stall A4, simmering since 1987.

Fourteen hours of bone and soy-bean broth, star anise, and a splash of shaoxing. Hand-pulled noodles stretched to order, five cuts of braised shank, and a spoon of chili oil that sneaks up on you.

200 bowls a night. When they're gone, they're gone.

How Hot Is It?

We asked every vendor to rate their signature dish, one to five chilies. Plan your night accordingly.

  1. 1 chili — Mild

    Golden Amber Pepper Buns — warm black pepper, no burn

  2. 2 chilies — Gentle

    Grandma Lin's Scallion Pancake — sweet soy dipping sauce

  3. 3 chilies — Warm

    Night Owl Stinky Tofu — pickled cabbage cuts the heat

  4. 4 chilies — Hot

    Jade Lantern Oyster Omelet — sweet chili glaze, real bite

  5. 5 chilies — Inferno

    Magenta Skewer House — five-spice chili oil, sign the waiver
